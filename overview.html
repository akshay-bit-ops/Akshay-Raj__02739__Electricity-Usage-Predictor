From CSV to forecast

A compact workflow that connects data cleaning, regression, prediction and visual explanation.

01 / INPUT

Upload monthly data

Provide a CSV containing month numbers and electricity units. Header and headerless formats are supported.

02 / ANALYSE

Fit a trend line

Validate records and use NumPy’s degree-one polynomial fit to estimate the linear relationship.

03 / OUTPUT

Review the estimate

See predicted units, estimated bill, regression metrics and a chart comparing actual and fitted usage.

The model, simply explained

The project uses simple linear regression as an educational forecasting baseline.

Prediction equation

Predicted Units = slope × Month + intercept

Estimated Bill = Predicted Units × ₹6

The slope and intercept are calculated from historical month/unit pairs using numpy.polyfit(month, units, 1).

What the result tells you

R² describes how closely the fitted line matches the observed data. It does not guarantee future accuracy. Predictions beyond the observed months are extrapolations and should be treated cautiously.

The ₹6/unit rate is an assignment assumption, not a complete real-world electricity tariff. Actual bills may include fixed charges, taxes and slab rates.
